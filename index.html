The tool you need might not exist yet

Sometimes you need a command that knows your repo’s conventions. Sometimes you need a panel to keep track of a long task. Describe it to Pixie, and it can build it into the session.

It can go beyond coding, too. Ask for a player to scrub through video takes, then a timeline to arrange them. Each request adds another piece to the tool you’re building.

Pixie can also spot repeated work and propose a tool for it. You decide which proposals to add.
